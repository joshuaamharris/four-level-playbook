Live maximum-two-trade rule

The edge comes from taking another approved signal after the first position is fully closed—not from overlapping positions or forcing a third trade.

Previous Day Low same-level recapture

Price tests Previous Day Low intrabar and then proves that buyers have reclaimed it on a completed candle.

EligibilityPrevious Day Low same-level recapture, with Previous Day High as the nearest higher Four-Level destination after entry.
What triggers the entry
  1. Test: During RTH, ES trades at or below Previous Day Low.
  2. Recapture: Wait for the first completed RTH-aligned 10-minute candle after the test to close at or above Previous Day Low.
  3. Enter: Buy at that completed candle’s closing price. Do not enter on the touch or before the candle closes.
2+20 points

Premarket Low failed breakdown / recapture

Price trades at least 1 point below the exact Premarket Low, traps breakdown sellers, and then reclaims the level on a completed candle.

EligibilityPremarket Low below Previous Day Low, at least 1 ES point below exact Premarket Low before recapture, and one of the two approved Four-Level stacks shown below.
What triggers the entry
  1. Breakdown: During RTH, ES trades at or below the exact Premarket Low.
  2. Depth: Before confirmation, the lowest price must reach at least 1.00 ES point below that exact level.
  3. Recapture: A completed RTH-aligned 10-minute candle closes at or above the exact Premarket Low.
  4. Enter: Buy at the confirming candle’s closing price.
3+4 points

Repeated shallow Premarket Low reaction

This is a later, separate shallow test of the exact Premarket Low—not the first reaction of the session.

EligibilityPremarket Low below Previous Day Low, a 0–1 ES point excursion below exact Premarket Low, a second-or-later separately armed reaction, and one of the two approved Four-Level stacks shown below.
What triggers the entry
  1. First reaction completes: An earlier Premarket Low test has already produced a completed 10-minute close back at or above the level.
  2. Reset: After that recapture, at least one 1-minute bar must trade completely above Premarket Low—its low is above the exact level.
  3. New shallow test: ES returns to Premarket Low and trades no more than 1.00 point below it.
  4. Recapture and enter: Buy at the close of the completed RTH-aligned 10-minute candle that closes back at or above exact Premarket Low.
